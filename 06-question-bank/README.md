# Question Bank

Practice scenarios for AI system design, framed as realistic situations — not bare "design X" prompts.

## Tracks

- [ML Questions](./ml-questions.md) — recommendation, search, fraud, ads
- [GenAI Questions](./genai-questions.md) — RAG, multi-tenant SaaS, chat, cost
- [Agentic Questions](./agentic-questions.md) — tool use, multi-agent, coding agents, voice
- [Cross-Cutting Questions](./cross-cutting-questions.md) — evaluation, security, drift, cost

## Format

Every question has:
- **Scenario** (2-4 sentences of realistic context)
- **Track, difficulty, and prerequisite chapters**
- **Your task** (the design question)
- **Worked answer** (the reasoning pattern, not just the final answer)

## Also on the website

These questions are also available interactively on the [website](../website/) — the Questions tab lets you filter by track and reveal worked answers.
