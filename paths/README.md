# Learning Paths — Choose Your On-Ramp

The course has one spine (Modules 0 → A → B → C → Cross-Cutting → Capstones, tracked in [PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md)), but different people arrive with different baggage. These on-ramps tell you what to skip, what to slow down on, and what your interview strengths and blind spots will be.

## How to use this

1. Find the description that fits you best below.
2. Read that path file fully — it maps your existing skills onto the course.
3. Follow its week-by-week plan. Each week names exact chapters and labs.
4. Track everything in [PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md).

## Which track do the jobs want?

The [AI Engineering Field Guide](https://github.com/alexeygrigorev/ai-engineering-field-guide) analyzed thousands of 2026 AI-engineer job postings and found (their numbers, their dataset): RAG appears in ~40% of AI-first postings, agents in ~55%, while fine-tuning is a focus of under 5%. The practical reading: **RAG + agents + evaluation + cost are the employable core**; classical ML depth is what makes you senior rather than junior. Our Track B, Track C, and the cross-cutting chapters are weighted accordingly.
