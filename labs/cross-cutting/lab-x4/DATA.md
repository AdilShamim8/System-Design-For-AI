**Calibration for self-grading (be honest; the rubric only works if you are):**

- First drafts, most people: failures and eval sections score 2 — the universal weak spots (that's why X.2–X.3 exist as chapters).
- Requirement sections drift toward 2 when you write "fast" instead of "p99 < 1.5s at 200 RPS" — the single most common downgrade.
- A doc where every "because" traces to a number *you measured* (your lab numbers, not this course's) is a 5 on data/model. Numbers borrowed from blogs are 3s wearing 5 clothing.

**The timer is part of the drill.** Open-ended writing produces beautiful docs and terrible interview training. 60 minutes, then stop — the constraint is the curriculum.

**License:** your docs, your license — and worth keeping public: a GitHub repo with two graded-and-rewritten design docs is a portfolio artifact almost no candidate has.
