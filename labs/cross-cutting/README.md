# Cross-Cutting Labs

The four labs that make systems operational — and one that makes you hirable. Each assumes its Track's core labs.

| # | Lab | Prereq | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [x1](./lab-x1/) | Monitoring and Drift Alerts | a7, b2 | 2 h |
| [x2](./lab-x2/) | The Cost Dashboard | b1, b5 | 2 h |
| [x3](./lab-x3/) | Load-Test Your AI Service | a1, x1 helpful | 2–3 h |
| [x4](./lab-x4/) | The Design-Document Drill | most labs | 3 h |

**The arc:** x1–x3 are the production trio (what to watch, what it costs, where it breaks). x4 is different: it's the *integration* lab — a real design doc under a timer, graded by the rubric, rewritten with your own numbers. Do x4 last; it's the bridge to [the mocks](../../interview-prep/mock-interviews/).
