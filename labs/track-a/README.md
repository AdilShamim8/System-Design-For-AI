# Track A — Classical ML Labs

The eight labs of Track A, in order. Each pairs with its chapters and assumes [setup](../setup/) is done. The rule: **your version runs before you open SOLUTION.md.**

| # | Lab | Real dataset | Prereq chapters | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [a1](./lab-a1/) | Your First Prediction Service | UCI Adult Income | 0.0, A.0, A.6 | 2–3 h |
| [a2](./lab-a2/) | Build a Recommendation Engine | MovieLens 100K | A.2 | 3–4 h |
| [a3](./lab-a3/) | Fraud Detection Under Imbalance | ULB Credit Card | A.5 | 2–3 h |
| [a4](./lab-a4/) | CTR Prediction and Calibration | Criteo (subsample) | A.4 | 2–3 h |
| [a5](./lab-a5/) | Search Ranking: BM25 vs Embeddings | ANTIQUE | A.3, B.3 | 2–3 h |
| [a6](./lab-a6/) | A Demand Forecasting Pipeline | NYC TLC Yellow | A.1, X.0 | 3–4 h |
| [a7](./lab-a7/) | The Drift Detective | Adult (shifted) | A.7, X.3 | 2 h |
| [a8](./lab-a8/) | A/B Testing Without Lying to Yourself | Simulation | X.2 | 2 h |

**The arc:** a1 makes a model meet HTTP; a2–a5 build the four classical ML product patterns (recommend, detect fraud, price ads, rank search); a6–a8 add the systems layer (point-in-time features, drift, experiments). After a8 you can design any classical ML system in [Mock 2, 3, 6](../../interview-prep/mock-interviews/).

**Journal reminder:** three sentences after every lab — what it does, where it breaks first, what you'd do next.
