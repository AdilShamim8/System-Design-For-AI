# 20 — The Interview Day Logistics Checklist

*The mechanical layer, on one page, so day-of cognition goes to the interview and not to parking. Print it.*

## The night before (T-12h)

- [ ] Route planned (onsite): door, not parking lot — add the walk; buffer for the lobby sign-in. Video: room booked/lit at the ACTUAL interview hour (light changes).
- [ ] Outfit decided and out (one notch above their daily code — check their engineering blog photos).
- [ ] Bag packed: printed resumes x5, water, snack (blood sugar is a cognition variable), charger, the day-of card (cheatsheet 12) printed.
- [ ] Phone: interviewer names + the loop schedule screenshotted (no morning app-scrolling to find it).
- [ ] Sleep alarm set for the 90-minute pre-round warm-up window (16's T-0 blocks) — sleep is the last study session.

## The morning (T-2h)

- [ ] Real breakfast; caffeine at YOUR normal time and dose (a double espresso you never drink is a tremor, not an edge).
- [ ] Warm-up done out loud: one bank section or one coding SOLUTION read aloud — the voice is a muscle.
- [ ] The 3-3-3: three deep breaths, your three best numbers, the three opening lines ("clarify, then design, then failure modes").

## Between rounds (onsite)

- [ ] Reset ritual per round: bathroom, water, one slow breath, the NEXT round's pre-brief row from cheatsheet 12.
- [ ] No post-morteming mid-day — it taxes the next round. Notes go on the back of a business card, max three words, revisit at 48h.
- [ ] Lunch is a round: curious questions about THEIR systems; perform zero.

## After (same evening)

- [ ] Thank-yous sent: one per interviewer, one sentence referencing the specific technical thread from THEIR round — not a template.
- [ ] The debrief row drafted in the tracker (15's five questions) — drafted, not interrogated.
- [ ] Laptop closed by dinner: the 48-hour rule applies to outcomes too.
