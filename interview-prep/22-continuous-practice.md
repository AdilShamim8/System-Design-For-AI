# 22 — The Continuous Practice Loop (Post-Offer, Forever)

*Selection isn't a one-time event — every internal transfer, promotion loop, and re-entry to the market runs on the same machinery. This is the maintenance schedule: how the interview layer stays sharp at 10 minutes a week, forever.*

## The weekly ten (pick two, ten minutes)

- **Deck rotation:** one deck per week through the ten (flashcards README's method — cover, say, uncover; misses go to your journal). The metrics, cost, and tells decks earn their rotation fastest.
- **One bank question, spoken:** pick any Q from the 67; answer it out loud in 5 minutes while doing dishes. The counter-intuitive rule: answer questions OUTSIDE your current domain — cross-domain recall is what decays first.

## The monthly hour

- **One cold mock (45 min) + one as interviewer (45 min):** the interviewer side is the maintenance people forget — grading keeps the rubric in your bones (the interviewer-guide's meta-protocol).
- **The numbers sheet refresh:** add THIS quarter's work numbers to your personal numbers page. Your STAR stories have a shelf life; new work renews them (the story bank's delivery rules apply to fresh material: 90 seconds, exact numbers, one reflection).

## The quarterly half-day

- **The market pulse (18's salary research, re-run):** levels.fyi bands for your level+market, posted ranges, one or two recruiter conversations even when happy — calibration is leverage, and 14's negotiation prep works only BEFORE you need it.
- **One real loop if promotion-minded:** internal transfers and promo committees use the same evidence structure — run yourself through the calibration guide's two-question check and close the gap BEFORE the cycle, not during.
- **The one-pager library skim:** the ten systems (see the one-pagers) are the durable canon — re-deriving one capacity math per quarter keeps the estimation reflexes warm (drills rot fastest).

## The re-entry protocol (when you decide to look again)

1. Week 1: tracker revived (19), loop-intelligence table filled for 5 targets, tiers re-checked (13) — the market moved; so should your aiming.
2. Week 2: 17-the-7-day-sprint's triage day, honestly scored — your gap now is not your gap then.
3. Weeks 3-4: practice-tier loops first, always — first impressions are non-renewable (13's rule, unchanged).

*The habit compounding note: candidates who interview WELL but stop practicing decay in ~6 months; candidates who keep the weekly ten decay never. Ten minutes a week is the cheapest career insurance that exists.*
