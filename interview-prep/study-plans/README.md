# Study Plans — Pick One and Commit

| Plan | For | Time | The shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| [30 days](./study-plan-30-days.md) | Interview on the calendar | 8–10 h/wk | Diagnose → weak-track repair → breadth → peak & taper. One spoken artifact per day, no exceptions. |
| [60 days](./study-plan-60-days.md) | Working engineer, 2-month horizon | 10–12 h/wk | 60% running the missing labs (interview material as a byproduct), 40% conversion. |
| [90 days](./study-plan-90-days.md) | Career-switcher / deeply rusty | 12–15 h/wk | The full spine + a capstone + interview mode. Month-3 exit: a deployed artifact and two "hire" mocks. |

**Choosing:** the honest question is "how many hours exist in my week," not "how ambitious am I" — the 30-day plan with real hours beats the 90-day plan with imaginary ones.

**The three constants in every plan:** flashcards 10 min/day · the lab journal (three sentences per lab) · one cold mock per month, recorded. The recordings are your progress curve.
