# The 90-Day Plan — The Full Transformation

**For:** the career-switcher or the deeply-rusty. From "I've read about this" to a portfolio with teeth. **12–15 hours/week.** This is the course's spine, honestly scheduled, with the capstone included.

## Month 1 — Foundations + Track A

- **Week 1:** Chapters 0.0–0.3 + setup + Lab a1 (serve your first model). Journal starts.
- **Week 2:** Chapters A.0–A.2 + Lab a2 (the recommender — the most-asked ML design).
- **Week 3:** Chapters A.4–A.5 + Labs a3 (fraud), a4 (CTR/calibration).
- **Week 4:** Chapters A.1, A.6–A.7 + Labs a6 (forecasting), a7 (drift). Flashcard habit: foundations deck, daily.

**Month-1 exit bar:** you can whiteboard the three-stage recommender and explain a cost-matrix threshold with your own numbers.

## Month 2 — Track B + the platform layer

- **Week 5:** Chapters B.0–B.2 + Labs b1 (cost), b2 (RAG from scratch — the flagship; take two sessions).
- **Week 6:** Chapters B.3–B.5 + Labs b3 (chunking), b5 (cache).
- **Week 7:** Chapters B.6, B.8 + Labs b4 (eval), b6 (fine-tune decision).
- **Week 8:** Chapters X.0–X.5 + Labs x1 (monitoring), x2 (cost dashboard). First mock at month end (Mock 1 or 4), cold — expect 40%; that's correct for this stage.

**Month-2 exit bar:** you can draw the full RAG pipeline with its failure modes and quote your own hit rates and cost curves.

## Month 3 — Agents + capstone + interview mode

- **Week 9:** Chapters C.0–C.2 + Labs c1 (agent loop), c2 (tool contracts).
- **Week 10:** Chapters C.3–C.5 + Lab c5 (agent eval). Read Mock 5 and 7 as case studies.
- **Weeks 11–12:** **The capstone (cap1 or cap3)** — the portfolio artifact. Follow its milestone gates; the final gate includes the x4 one-pager.
- **Week 13:** Full interview mode: banks (your track + bridge), drills 1–12, three mocks, field notes all 12, seniority ladder, behavioral stories. Follow the 30-day plan's week 4 for the final stretch.

**Month-3 exit bar:** a deployed capstone with an eval report + two mocks at "hire" on your own honest grading.

## The three habits that carry all 90 days

1. **Flashcards 10 min/day** from week 2 on (all four decks in rotation by month 2).
2. **The journal** — three sentences per lab; by month 3 it's your story bank (behavioral round, solved).
3. **One mock per month, cold** — the recordings are your progress curve. Day 28's 40% and day 88's "hire" are the same person, measured.

## If you slip (you will)

The plan degrades gracefully: cut extensions first, then the optional labs (a5, a8→skim, b7, b8), never the spine (a1–a3, b1–b4, c1, x1–x2, one capstone). A finished spine beats an abandoned whole — in coursework as in systems.
