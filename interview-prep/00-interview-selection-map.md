## Route by level (the ladder, abridged)

- **Junior → mid:** the labs' numbers replace adjectives — flashcards decks 1–4, coding problems, banks round 1.
- **Mid → senior:** round-2 banks (the operations questions), STAR + deep-dive defense, mocks 9–12, the calibration guide.
- **Senior → staff:** Mock 12 (the platform), Q-CC-10–14 (the business boundary), the interviewer's guide — grade someone; you'll hear the levels.

## Route by gap (the calibration guide's one-gap diagnosis)

- No numbers → deck 5 (metrics) + your lab numbers sheet + drill banks 07/07b daily.
- Structure collapse → framework + three structure-scored mocks + cheatsheet 12's recovery moves.
- Deep-dive folding → 09b's five patterns, mapped to your strongest design; mock-as-interviewer.
- Can't build → coding 1–8, one per day, narrated.

## The two habits that decide selection

1. **Out loud or it didn't happen** — every drill in this layer is spoken, timed, recorded when possible. Recognition is not production.
2. **Numbers over adjectives** — you have what no other candidate has: measured numbers from labs you ran. Every artifact in this layer exists to put those numbers in your mouth at the right moment.
