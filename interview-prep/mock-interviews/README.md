**Recommended order:** your own domain second (confidence), your weakest domain third (growth), Mock 4 or 1 somewhere in the middle (most transferable). Mock 1 and 4 are the commonest real-world prompts.

Each mock embeds 3–4 curveballs from [08-curveball-questions.md](../08-curveball-questions.md) — the walkthrough answers show the *shape* of thinking under a shove, which is the actual interview skill.
