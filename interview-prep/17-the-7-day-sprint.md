# The 7-Day Interview Sprint — "I Have an Interview Thursday"

**What this is:** the panic protocol — you have 5–10 days before a real loop, and the 30/60/90 plans don't fit. The sprint triages YOUR gap, then drills the highest-leverage artifacts per day. It assumes the course content exists and is 60–80% learned; the sprint's job is sequencing and rehearsal, not teaching.

## Day 0 (2 hours, tonight): the triage

Take one mock cold (pick the round type you're WEAKEST at — if you don't know, take mock 4, the most general). Score it honestly with the calibration guide. Your worst rubric dimension decides the week's shape:

- **No numbers anywhere** → structure A (the numbers problem: you're technically fine, evidence-poor)
- **Structure collapse** (rambling, no spine) → structure B (the framework problem)
- **Deep-dive folding** (can't go level 2 on your own design) → structure C (the depth problem)
- **Can't build the thing** (coding round fumbled) → structure D (the hands problem)

## Structure A — the numbers problem (most common)

| Day | Drill (2–4h/day) |
|---|---|
| 1 | The flashcards-numbers deck twice + your own lab numbers sheet (one page: every measurement you have) |
| 2 | Round-2 ML bank, 5 answers OUT LOUD, each answer must volunteer ≥3 numbers |
| 3 | Round-2 GenAI bank + 06-llm-cost-math cheatsheet; re-derive your labs' cost math |
| 4 | Estimation drills 07 (all 12) — the muscle of deriving numbers live |
| 5 | One mock, interviewer-scored: the rubric row "numbers volunteered" must pass |
| 6 | The weak round's round-2 bank, one more pass + cheatsheet #12 pre-brief |
| 7 | Taper: 72-hour protocol takes over |

## Structure B — the framework problem

| Day | Drill |
|---|---|
| 1 | The 6-step framework memorized as QUESTIONS not steps ("what's the job? what's the budget? what's the data?") |
| 2–3 | Three mocks, one per track (ML/GenAI/agents), each scored for structure only — did the spine appear in the first 5 minutes? |
| 4 | The curveball bank + the three recovery moves from cheatsheet #12 (structure under surprise IS the framework, stressed) |
| 5 | Mock #4, full 45 min, recorded; listen for filler-to-content ratio |
| 6 | STAR pass + behavioral round-2 (structure collapse usually infects behavioral too) |
| 7 | Taper (72-hour protocol) |

## Structure C — the depth problem

| Day | Drill |
|---|---|
| 1 | The deep-dive defense guide's 5 attack patterns; map each to your strongest design |
| 2 | For your target track's design: write the 3 "why"s you can't currently answer (why this DB? why this latency budget? why not the obvious alternative?) — then answer them from the labs' numbers |
| 3 | Mock as INTERVIEWER (the guide): you'll feel where candidates go shallow because you went shallow |
| 4 | The seniority ladder re-read + record yourself answering ONE question at each level |
| 5 | Full mock, demand the interviewer deep-dive mercilessly; score only the recovery moves |
| 6 | Round-2 bank for the OTHER track (breadth insurance — depth candidates often over-index one domain) |
| 7 | Taper (72-hour protocol) |

## Structure D — the hands problem

| Day | Drill |
|---|---|
| 1 | Coding deck + problems 1 and 3 (the leak family — highest-yield pattern) |
| 2 | Problems 2 and 6 (precompute discipline + PSI) |
| 3 | Problem 7 (the loop — read the solution, then rebuild from memory) |
| 4 | Problem 8 (eval harness — the one that maps to takehomes too) |
| 5 | Problems 4, 5 (cache policy, chunking stats — the QA-numbers angle) |
| 6 | Redo your TWO worst from memory, timed; narration mandatory |
| 7 | Taper (72-hour protocol) |

## The sprint's rules

1. **Out loud or it didn't happen.** Reading banks silently builds recognition; interviews need production. Every drill above is spoken.
2. **One artifact per day is written down** (a number sheet, a why-answer, a scored rubric) — the sprint must leave residue, or the NEXT sprint starts from zero.
3. **Sleep is non-negotiable from day 5.** The 72-hour protocol's sleep rule overrides any incomplete drill.
4. **The sprint is triage, not mastery.** Its success metric: your weakest rubric row passes the next real loop. The 30/60/90 plans are what make sprints unnecessary — start one the week AFTER the interview.
