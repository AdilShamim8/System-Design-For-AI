# 19 — The Application & Pipeline Tracker

*A working template, not advice: the spreadsheet-shaped habit that turns a job search from a scroll into a pipeline. Copy the tables into your own tracker (Notion, Sheets, a notebook — the medium doesn't matter; the columns do).*

## The pipeline table (one row per application)

| # | Company | Tier (13) | Role | Applied | Source | Status | Next step | Date of next | Loop shape known? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | (example) AcmeAI | AI-native | AI Systems Eng | Nov 3 | referral | phone scheduled | tech phone | Nov 12 | yes: design + coding + behavioral |

**Status vocabulary (closed set — no vibes):** `applied · recruiter-screen · tech-phone · onsite · offer · rejected · withdrawn · ghosted (2wk+ silence)`.

**The rules that make the table work:**

1. **Every row has a next step and a date, always.** A row without one is dead weight; "waiting" is a status, not a plan.
2. **Ghosted is a status after 14 days, not a feeling** — one polite nudge at day 10, mark ghosted at 14, move on (15's rejection protocol).
3. **The funnel math lives at the bottom:** applications → screens → onsites → offers. At the realistic rates (30 percent screen-pass, 20-30 percent onsite-pass), you need 8-10 live processes for one offer — the table tells you when you're below the funnel, before the calendar does.
4. **Tier balance check weekly:** practice / pipeline / stretch (13's aiming strategy) — if everything is stretch, you've built an anxiety generator; if everything is pipeline, you've built a comfort zone.

## The loop-intelligence table (one row per company, filled as you learn)

| Company | Round structure | Known bar-raisers? | Their AI surface | My angle (1 sentence) | Questions I want to ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

*Fill this BEFORE the recruiter call — their product's AI surface (who is their AI for? what's the obvious failure mode?) is your 'why us' answer and your question list (the strong three from the process deck).*

## The weekly review (Sunday, 20 minutes)

- Moved rows: which next-steps fired, which expired?
- Funnel: screens this week? If zero for two weeks, the top of the funnel is starving — applications beat contemplation.
- Energy: which rows am I dreading? Dread is data — usually a team-fit mismatch you already sense (14's team-over-company rule).
